# Rider sprite prompts v3 (16-bit, sneaky)

## 1. Character model sheet (1536x1024)

```
Pixel art character model sheet, landscape 1536x1024.
Style: 16-bit console-era pixel art (SNES / Mega Drive look). Chunky visible pixels on a clean grid: one art pixel = a 4x4 block of image pixels, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur, no painterly shading. Limited palette, about 24 colours per character, flat cel shading in 2 or 3 tones per material, 1-art-pixel dark outline. Simplify fine detail: folds, harness rings and buckles are suggested by one or two pixels, the face reads from a few well-placed pixels.
Character: the woman in the attached reference photos, as a 16-bit caricature: slightly enlarged head, blonde shoulder-length hair with volume and outward-curling ends, side parting, light green eyes, strong jaw, slightly drooping eyelids. Age in her late fifties.
Outfit and props: navy suit jacket and trousers, white shirt, black kitesurf harness with a red accent, black sandals, white board with red and blue edges, blue control bar held in both hands, 4 thin white kite lines leaving the bar towards the top right corner of the cell, cut 120 px above the bar (the kite is drawn separately).
Top row, full body standing, about 420 px tall, feet on the same invisible baseline: front view, three-quarter view facing right, side view facing right, back view. Neutral poker face.
Bottom row, five head-and-shoulders close-ups, about 200 px tall, all facing three-quarter right: 1) neutral poker face, half-lidded; 2) sly sideways glance, eyes to the right, lips closed; 3) small smug crooked half-smile, one corner of the mouth up; 4) scheming look, narrowed eyes, faint smirk, one eyebrow slightly raised; 5) mild annoyance, lips pressed. No broad smile, no shouting.
Flat solid background #1B2433, no gradient, no ground, no shadow. At least 60 px of empty background between figures. No text, numbers, logos or borders.
```
