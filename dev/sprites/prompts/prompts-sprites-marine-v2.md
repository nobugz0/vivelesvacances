# Rider sprite sheets v2: prompts for ChatGPT Images

## How to use

- One prompt per new ChatGPT conversation. Image size: **landscape 1536 × 1024**.
- Attach your reference images of the character (face, hair, build) to every prompt.
- Generate sheet 1 first. Once it looks right, attach the approved sheet 1 as an extra reference to every following prompt (sheets 2 to 6 and the illustrations): it locks the face, outfit, pixel density and scale.
- If the output adds a sea, a sky, a reflection, text or a gradient, reply: "Same image, flat #1B2433 background only, nothing else."
- Send me the sheets as they come; I cut them, align the feet and rebuild the atlas.

## Shared art direction (already included in each prompt)

- Detailed pixel art, crisp 1-px dark outlines, saturated colours, light from the top left, no blur, no painterly shading.
- The character from the attached reference images: same face and hair. Outfit (described in full, no outfit reference): navy suit jacket and trousers, white shirt, black kitesurf harness with red accents and silver rings, black sandals, white board with red and blue edges, blue control bar held in both hands, 4 thin white kite lines leaving the bar towards the top right corner of the cell and cut 120 px above the bar (the kite is drawn separately in the game).
- Attitude: **no smile.** Jaw clenched, brows lowered and drawn together, narrowed eyes locked forward, chin pushed out, shoulders squared. Effort frames: teeth bared in a snarl. The overall read is combative and ruthless, not cheerful.
- Side view, facing **right**. Same character scale in every frame of every sheet: about 300 px from the soles to the top of the hair.
- Layout: 3 columns × 2 rows of equal cells (512 × 512 each). One frame per cell, centred horizontally, the board's underside on the same invisible baseline in every cell of a row, at least 60 px of empty background around each frame.
- Flat solid background #1B2433, no gradient, no ground, no sea surface, no reflection, no shadow on the background. Any water spray is small, white, and cut flat at the board's underside. No text, no numbers, no logos, no frame borders.
